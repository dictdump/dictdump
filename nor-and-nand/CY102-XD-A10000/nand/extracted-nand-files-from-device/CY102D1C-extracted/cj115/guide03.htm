Terms, symbols and short forms

&F044; AmE

American English

&F044; BrE

British English

&F044; formal

used in formal contexts, such as writing a report or speaking at a conference

&F044; informal

used in informal contexts, such as conversation with family or friends

&F044; noun, verb, adjective, adverb

the part that a word plays in its sentence (Note: these labels are not used in all cases, but only when they are needed for clarity.)

&F044; U

an uncountable noun

&F044; C/U

a noun that is often countable but can also be uncountable

&F044; U/C

a noun that is often uncountable but can also be countable

&F044; singular

a noun that is used only in the singular

&F044; plural

a noun that is used only in the plural

&F044; with singular or plural verb

a singular noun (for example family, government) which can have a plural verb if you are thinking of the actions, feelings, etc of the induvidual members of the group

&F044; only before a noun

an adjective which comes before a noun, not after a verb like is or seems

&F044; not before a noun

an adjective which comes after a verb like is or seems, not before a noun

&F044; usually passive

a verb that is more common in the passive than the active form

&F044; abbreviation

a short form of a word

&F044; opposite

a word which is opposite in meaning to another word

&F044; sb

somebody

&F044; sth

something
