WORD CHOICE(ワード･チョイス)

　LDOCE第4版のコラムの中で,1000万語のロングマン学習者コーパス(Longman Learner's Corpus)を活用したのが,このWORD CHOICEである｡ここでは,学習者が間違えやすい単語の使い方に関してエラー情報をうまく活用しながらアドバイスをしている｡例としてfaultを見てみよう｡


fault&F095; &F149;&F14E; /f&F053;&F05E;lt $ f&F052;&F05E;lt/ n [C]


[BOX] WORD CHOICE: fault, blame, mistake

If someone causes something bad, you can say that it is their fault or that they are to blame

¶The accident was my fault.

¶Nobody is to blame for what happened.

&F147; Do not say that someone 'has the/a etc fault'

¶We didn't think that it was our fault OR that we were to blame (NOT that we had any fault).

&F147; Do not say that something is 'someone's blame'.

Use fault to mean something that is wrong with a machine or system, or something that you could criticize about a person or thing

¶The car engine had developed a fault.

¶The book's only fault is that it is too long.

Use mistake to mean something that is wrong in someone's grammar, spelling, calculations, decisions etc

¶Please correct any mistakes (NOT faults) in my letter.


　ここでは｢(何か悪いことが起きたのは)彼らのせいだ｣という場合に, it is their fault または they are to blame という言い方を示している｡それと同時に次の2つのタイプのエラーが示されている:

&F97E;　someone ‘has the/a etc fault'

(→　正解は it is someone's fault)

&F97E;　something is ‘someone's blame'

(→　正解はsomeone is to blame)

　この2例とも,ロングマン学習者コーパスの実際のエラー例から取ったものだ｡事実,コラムに取り上げられているテーマは,微妙な意味の違いや使い分けが難しい項目を適切に選択して,エラー･データを基に実にうまく解説されており,心にくいばかりである｡

　このほかワード･チョイスに取り上げられている項目には,次のようなものがある｡

●say, tell, give, ask の違い

●excuse me, pardon me, beg your pardon, sorry の違い

●direct, take, guide, lead の違い

●close, shut, lock, turn off の違い

●lastly, finally, eventually, in the end, at last の違い
