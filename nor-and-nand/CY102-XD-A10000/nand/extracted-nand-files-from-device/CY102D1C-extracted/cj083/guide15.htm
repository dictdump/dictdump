Formal and informal


Register information (whether a word is formal or informal) is given when any pair of words in combination takes on a different register from the two words separately. Examples would be do drugs (informal) &F145; though neither do nor drugs is informal in itself &F145; or hear a lecture (formal). Collocations are also labelled if they belong to a particular field of language such as law or medicine. For a full list of the usage labels used in this dictionary, see Labels used in the dictionary. In addition to these labels, more specific usage restrictions such as in football or used in journalism are given in brackets.
