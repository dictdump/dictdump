curling

-- definition --

[mass noun] a game played on ice, especially in Scotland and Canada, in which large round flat stones are slid across the surface towards a mark.

-- additional information --

Members of a team use brooms to sweep the surface of the ice in the path of the stone to control its speed and direction.


cuttlebone

-- definition --

&F252;noun the flattened oval internal skeleton of the cuttlefish, which is made of white lightweight chalky material.

-- additional information --

It is used as a dietary supplement for cage birds and for making casts for precious metal items.
