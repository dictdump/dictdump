そのほかの語源に関する特徴としては､｢言語内語源｣と｢民間語源｣を含むということが挙げられる｡言語内語源は見出し語の中に示してあり､外国語に由来しない成句や慣用句の意味とその起源を説明する｡例えば､ red herring の比喩的意味はどのようにして生じたのだろうか? また､なぜ一時的に成功した人を flash in the pan と呼ぶのだろうか?


red herring

-- definition --

&F253;a dried smoked herring, which is turned red by the smoke.

&F254;a clue or piece of information which is or is intended to be misleading or distracting

-- origin --

[origin so named from the practice of using the scent of red herring in training hounds.]


flash&F0DB;

-- definition --

flash in the pan a thing or person whose sudden but brief success is not repeated or repeatable

-- origin --

[origin with allusion to priming of a firearm, the flash arising from an explosion of gunpowder within the lock.]
