General meaning, showing what all the verbs have in common

These verbs are all used to talk about buildings and other structures falling to the ground.

Definition of each verb

collapse (of a building, etc.) to fall down or fall in suddenly, often after breaking apart: The roof collapsed under the weight of snow.

Which word? section, outlining some of the important differences between the verbs

WHICH WORD?

Only fall down and collapse can be used to talk about whole buildings.
