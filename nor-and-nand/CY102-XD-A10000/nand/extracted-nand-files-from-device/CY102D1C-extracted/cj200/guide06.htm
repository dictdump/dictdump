Stress in phrasal verbs

In speech it is important to use the correct stress patterns for phrasal verbs. To help with this, verbs are shown in this dictionary with stresses marked using the symbols /&F06D;/ (= a main stress) and /&F06E;/ (= a weaker secondary stress).
