｢月刊英語が聞こえる耳｣　2005年11月号 News 2 P2/3

Toyota and Fuji Heavy plan to respect each other's brands
and management, the sources said.
They will make a clear distinction between Fuji Heavy's
Subaru vehicles and Toyota brand vehicles. They will not use
the same dealers to sell both brands. In addition, Toyota
has no plans to send executives to Fuji Heavy.
Fuji Heavy on Oct. 5 said it will sever its capital
alliance with struggling General Motors Corp. of the United
States. Toyota will become Fuji Heavy's top shareholder by
buying 8.7 percent of the 20 percent of Fuji Heavy shares
held by GM.
