｢月刊英語が聞こえる耳｣　2005年7月号 News 2 P3/3

According to the ruling, Mori, then 26, worked at Kansai
Medical University Hospital in 1998 usually from 7:30 a.m.
to past midnight during weekdays. He was also forced to work
mornings on weekends and sometimes stayed overnight at the
hospital as an assistant night shift doctor. He worked an
average of 114 hours a week, although the legal standard is
40 hours.
After complaining of health problems to his father, Mori
died on Aug. 16.
(Read by Mari Yamada)
