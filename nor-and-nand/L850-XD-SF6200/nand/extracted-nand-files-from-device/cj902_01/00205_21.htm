EU、2007年27カ国にルーマニア・ブルガリアが条約調印
Bulgaria, Romania Sign EU Accession Accords

(AP) ― Bulgaria and Romania signed their accession treaties
with the European Union, setting the stage for their entry
into the bloc in 2007 if they complete the necessary
political and economic reforms on time.
The EU insisted on a clause in the treaties allowing the
bloc to delay the entry of either country by a year if
needed reforms are not enacted. Leaders from the two former
Communist countries signed their respective treaties on
April 25 at the old Neumuenster Abbey in the historic heart
of Luxembourg city.
