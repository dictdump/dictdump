パレスチナ自治政府議長選､アッバス氏が勝利
Abbas Wins Decisive Victory in Palestinian Election

(AP) ― Pragmatist Mahmoud Abbas was elected Palestinian
Authority president by a wide margin on Jan. 9, winning a
decisive mandate to renew peace talks with Israel, rein in
militants and try to end more than four years of Mideast
bloodshed. The victory of the staid Abbas, who has spoken
out against violence and has the backing of the
international community, ushered in a new era, after four
decades of chaotic and corruption-riddled rule by Yasser
Arafat who died on Nov. 11.
