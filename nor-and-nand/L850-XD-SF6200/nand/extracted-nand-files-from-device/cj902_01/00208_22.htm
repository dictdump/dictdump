｢月刊英語が聞こえる耳｣　2005年3月号 News 2 P2/3

“He's only the messenger!” one of the members of the
Punxsutawney Groundhog Club - the volunteer group in charge
of Phil and the town's Groundhog Day festivities - reminded
the crowd braving the frigid weather. The tradition that
began in 1886 stems from the Christian holiday of Candlemas,
and the belief that if a hibernating animal sees its shadow,
winter will last another six weeks. If there's no shadow,
spring will come early. Since the tradition began, Phil saw
his shadow for the 95th time.
