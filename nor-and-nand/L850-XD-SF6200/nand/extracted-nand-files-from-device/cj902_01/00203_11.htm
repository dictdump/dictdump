3 Ex-Kanebo Execs Held in Earnings Flap
粉飾決算指示の疑いでカネボウ元社長ら逮捕

(The Asahi Shimbun) ― Tokyo prosecutors on July 29 arrested
a former president of Kanebo Ltd. and two past executives on
suspicion they instructed subordinates to inflate company
earnings in financial statements.
All three likely will be charged with violating the
Securities and Exchange Law, sources said.
Kanebo, established more than a century ago and once a
textile industry giant, is currently working to revive its
operations under the supervision of the Industrial
Revitalization Corp. of Japan.
