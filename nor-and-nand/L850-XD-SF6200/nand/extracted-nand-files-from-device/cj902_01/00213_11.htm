愛想のつもりが迷惑行為､共存の道探る

(AP) ― A “playful” killer whale who likes to frolic
alongside fishermen has damaged three boats in separate
recent incidents. Luna, described by fishermen as a friendly
5-year-old whale, has made frequent contact with people and
boats in waters off Vancouver Island, about 200 kilometers
north of the U.S. border, since he began frequenting the
waters more than two years ago. Now Canadian officials and
an Indian group that believes the animal is the
reincarnation of its late chief are working on a plan to
protect both Luna and humans. Officials hope to eventually
reunite him with his pod of U.S. relatives.
