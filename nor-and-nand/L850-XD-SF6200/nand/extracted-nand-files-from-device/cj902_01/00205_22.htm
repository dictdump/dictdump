｢月刊英語が聞こえる耳｣　2005年6月号 News 2 P2/2

“The truth is that Bulgaria and Romania ... have not known
freedom as we have” in post-war Europe.
“Today we put an end to that,” said Luxembourg Prime
Minister Jean-Claude Juncker in a speech ahead of the
signing. 　
The EU declared Romania a market economy only last June,
and to join the bloc in 2007 it must eradicate corruption,
cut down on state aid, boost justice and law enforcement,
respect the rights of its Roma minority and improve
environmental protection and antitrust rules.
Bulgaria must get a handle on corruption, adopt a new penal
code and combat organized crime.
(Read by Cheryl Meyers)
