｢月刊英語が聞こえる耳｣　2005年10月号 News 1 P2/2

According to the sources, officials from the Afghan
Ministry of Health used dental records to identify the two
after the bodies were transported to Kabul from the Daman
district near Kandahar, where they had been found. Both were
clad in T-shirts and each had been shot once in the head.
A doctor who examined the bodies said the bullets had been
fired from close range, likely more than three weeks ago.
Authorities suspect the two may have been victims of robbery
as no militant group has so far come forward to take
responsibility for the killings. They noted the area was the
scene of growing civil unrest in the countdown to the
country's Sept. 18 parliamentary election.
(Read by Mari Yamada)
