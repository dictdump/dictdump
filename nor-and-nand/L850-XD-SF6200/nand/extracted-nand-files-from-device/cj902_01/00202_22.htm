｢月刊英語が聞こえる耳｣　2005年10月号 News 2 P2/3

The LDP appeared strong in urban and most other areas, with
the poll showing that the ruling party could win about 250
of the 480 seats in the Lower House. Meanwhile, opposition
Minshuto (Democratic Party of Japan) appears unlikely to
regain its pre-election strength of 177 seats. Minshuto is
fielding the largest number of candidates ever in the
single-seat districts in an attempt to gain control of the
government, but it appears likely that it will fall short of
the 105 single seats won in the last election.
