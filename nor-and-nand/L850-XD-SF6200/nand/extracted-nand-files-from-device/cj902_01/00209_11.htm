Korean Edges French in Language Classes

(The Asahi Shimbun) ― In what might be seen as a
grass-roots good neighbor policy, Korean has slipped past
French as the second most popular optional language to be
taught in Japanese high schools. An Asahi Shimbun survey
showed an increase of 28 schools in the 2003-04 school year
that offered the language, which is syntactically close to
Japanese. A total of 247 public or private high schools are
teaching 6,960 students Korean this year, according to the
December survey that covered the school year ending March
31, 2005. English is a compulsory subject at most high
schools.
