Textbooks Heavy on Advanced Content

(The Asahi Shimbun) － While China and South Korea have made
their objections clear to newly approved textbooks for use
in junior high schools, educators and others take pride in
the “advanced” content of the tomes. They said the science
and math texts that passed screening by the education
ministry go beyond approved curriculum guidelines. This,
they said, reflected moves by publishers to appease parents'
concerns about the level of education their children are
receiving following curriculum changes enacted in fiscal
2002 that cut about 30 percent of content from all courses.
