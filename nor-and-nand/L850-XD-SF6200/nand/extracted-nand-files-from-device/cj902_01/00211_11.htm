(米国)地雷原→実り豊かな大地へ!
｢平和の味｣普及を目指し､世界各地で活動

(AP) ―　Heidi Kuhn tasted the fruits of her labors in
September when she visited Croatia and sampled the first
harvest from a vineyard that used to be a minefield.
“We ate the grapes,” she says.
“We tasted peace.” Kuhn is the founder of Roots of Peace,
a humanitarian organization that is working to clear land
mines and turn the earth back into farmland from Cambodia to
Croatia - a project that has drawn the support of several
vintners in California's Napa Valley.
