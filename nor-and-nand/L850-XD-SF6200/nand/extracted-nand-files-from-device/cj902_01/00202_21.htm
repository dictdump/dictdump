Poll: LDP Could be Headed for a Majority
総選挙情勢は自民優勢､過半数うかがう

(The Asahi Shimbun) ― Prime Minister Junichiro Koizumi and
his Liberal Democratic Party postal privatization brigade
may be heading for an outright majority in the Sept. 11
Lower House election, according to a poll conducted by The
Asahi Shimbun. It would be the first such majority since
1990.
The nationwide telephone poll was conducted between
Aug. 31, the day after the official start of the campaign,
and Sept. 3. A total of 187,897 households with a registered
voter were called at random, and valid responses were
received from 118,616 voters. Voter interest in the election
was high, with nearly 80 percent saying they would vote.
