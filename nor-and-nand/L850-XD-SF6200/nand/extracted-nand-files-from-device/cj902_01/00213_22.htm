Earlier this year, the couple read a newspaper article
about the Never Too Late Foundation, which grants wishes to
the elderly. They contacted the group and told them their
wish was to jump out of an airplane. “We woke up one morning
and he said, ‘We're boring. We don't do anything exciting
anymore,’ ” Esther recalled her husband saying. But the
couple has already been whitewater rafting and parasailing
before their Aug. 27 jump. “They were just made for each
other,” said longtime friend Mary Hershberger, who was
Esther's maid of honor in 1944.
