｢月刊英語が聞こえる耳｣　2005年10月号 News 2 P3/3

The actual results of the election could still differ
widely from the poll results because 40 percent of the
respondents said they still had not decided who to vote for
in the single-seat districts. Meanwhile, 30 percent of
respondents said they were undecided about which party to
vote for in the proportional representation constituency.
(Read by Jason Tester)
