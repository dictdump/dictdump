｢月刊英語が聞こえる耳｣　2005年3月号 News 1 P1/2
(オーストリア)ウィーン社交界にも禁煙の波
喫煙者用｢吸入器｣やガムまでお目見え

(AP) ―　At Vienna's famed Opera Ball this year, Strauss was
definitely in, while smoking was most definitely out. For
the first time in its 70-year history, Vienna's Opera Ball
outlawed smoking at its Feb. 3 gala event. But it was a
genteel ban, in keeping with the spirit of the ball that
draws the rich and famous from around the world. The
organizers, taking pity on their tobacco-addicted guests,
handed out nicotine gum and cigarette-shaped inhalers at the
cloakroom for those who needed a fix.
