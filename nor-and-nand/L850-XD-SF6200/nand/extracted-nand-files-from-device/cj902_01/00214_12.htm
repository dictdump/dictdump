｢月刊英語が聞こえる耳｣　2005年8月号 News 1 P2/2

In London, a thunderous roar erupted from the
200,000-strong crowd as the two iconic performers, Paul
McCartney and George Michael, belted out the first line of
“Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band”: “It was 20 years
ago today...” - a nod to the mammoth Live Aid concerts that
raised millions for African famine relief two decades ago.
This time, the scale was bigger - 10 concerts instead of two
and, thanks to the Internet, a potential audience of
billions. The goal was also different: “We don't want your
money,” said Live 8 banners in London. “We want you.”
Organizer Bob Geldof promised to deliver “the greatest
concert ever.” Crowd estimates taken at Live 8 venues
indicated more than 1 million people attended the shows.
(Read by Kelly Haavaldsrud)
