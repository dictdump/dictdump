｢月刊英語が聞こえる耳｣　2005年9月号 News 1 P2/2

The Tokyo District Public Prosecutors Office is convinced
the former executives played a leading role in falsifying
the company's financial statements, investigative sources
said.
An in-house probe found that the company inflated its
earnings by at least 215 billion yen over five years to
fiscal 2003. Of that figure, the company has been found to
have inflated its sales by a total of about 38 billion yen
for fiscal 2001 and 2002 to conceal its negative net worth.
(Read by Mari Yamada)
