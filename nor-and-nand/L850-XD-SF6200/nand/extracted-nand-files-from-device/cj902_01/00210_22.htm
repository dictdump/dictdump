Howes then spotted what he described as a 3-meter great

he said. Howes realized what the dolphins were doing: “They
had corralled us up to protect us.” Another lifeguard, Matt
Fleet, on patrol in a lifeboat, saw the dolphins circling
the swimmers and slapping their tails on the water to keep
them in place. Fleet told the newspaper he had a clear
sighting of the shark. Ingrid Visser, of the group Orca
Research, said that the dolphins probably sensed the humans
were in danger and took action to protect them.
