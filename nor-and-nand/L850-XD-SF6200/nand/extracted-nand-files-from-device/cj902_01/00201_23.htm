｢月刊英語が聞こえる耳｣　2005年11月号 News 2 P3/3

Fuji Heavy has been preparing to launch a hybrid version of
its popular Legacy passenger car in fiscal 2007. The company
now plans to end development of major components, such as
electric motors, and instead buy them from Toyota to reduce
costs, the sources said.
(Read by Brock Spore)
