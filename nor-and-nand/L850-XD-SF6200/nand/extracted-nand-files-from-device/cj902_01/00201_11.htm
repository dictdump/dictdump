Peace Prize Honors ElBaradei and U.N. Agency on Nuclear Arms
エルバラダイ氏とIAEAにノーベル平和賞

(AP) ― Mohamed ElBaradei and the International Atomic
Energy Agency that he heads were awarded the 2005 Nobel
Peace Prize on Oct. 7 for their efforts to prevent the
proliferation of nuclear weapons.
ElBaradei, an Egyptian lawyer, has led the U.N. nuclear
agency as it grappled with the crises in Iraq and the
continuing efforts to prevent North Korea and Iran from
acquiring nuclear weapons.
“Everyone who has contributed to the IAEA has a part in
this important prize,” the Nobel committee chairman, Ole
Danbolt Mjoes, said as he announced the prize.
