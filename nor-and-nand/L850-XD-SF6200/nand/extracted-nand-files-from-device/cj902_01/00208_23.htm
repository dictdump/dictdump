｢月刊英語が聞こえる耳｣　2005年3月号 News 2 P3/3

He hasn't seen his shadow 14 times; nine years have no
record of the outcome, according to the Punxsutawney club.
Whether Phil sees his shadow is beside the point, however.
Organizers of the event and the festivals, concerts and
craft fair in this town 105 kilometers northeast of
Pittsburgh say Groundhog Day is all about the fun - and a
chance to shake winter's chilly cloak.
(Read by Jason Tester)
