Bodies Identified as Missing Teachers
アフガニスタンで不明の2教諭､遺体発見

(The Asahi Shimbun) ― Two bodies found in a ditch near
Afghanistan's southern border with Pakistan on Sept. 1 have
been confirmed as those of two missing Japanese teachers
from Hiroshima Prefecture, Foreign Ministry sources said on
Sept. 3.
Jun Fukusho, 44, and Shinobu Hasegawa, 30, colleagues at
Miki Junior High School in Onomichi, were last spotted
nearly a month ago getting into a taxi after passing through
a border checkpoint.
