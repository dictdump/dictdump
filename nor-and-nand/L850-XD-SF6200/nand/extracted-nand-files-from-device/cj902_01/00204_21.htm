Court Gives Medical Interns Workers Status
最高裁｢研修医は労働者｣認定､賃金支払い命令

(The Asahi Shimbun) ― The Supreme Court on June 3 ruled
that medical interns are workers, not students, a
precedent-setting decision that will likely change the
“slave labor” conditions facing trainee doctors.
The top court upheld the ruling of the Osaka High Court
that ordered Kansai Medical University Hospital to pay
\420,000 to the bereaved parents of Hirohito Mori, an intern
at the hospital for about two months before he died of a
heart attack in 1998.
