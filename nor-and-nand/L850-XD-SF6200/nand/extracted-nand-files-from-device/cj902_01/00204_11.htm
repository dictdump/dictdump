Britain Kills Vote on EU Constitution
イギリス､EU憲法条約の国民投票を棚上げ

(AP) ― Britain on June 6 shelved a referendum on the
European Union constitution, following the document's
rejection in France and the Netherlands.
The move strongly suggests that the treaty in its current
form cannot be revived, and removes a major complication
for Prime Minister Tony Blair, whose euro-skeptic electorate
would have been difficult to convince.
