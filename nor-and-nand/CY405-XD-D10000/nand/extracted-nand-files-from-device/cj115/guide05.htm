Geographical names

This list shows the English spelling of geographical names. If a country has a different word for the adjective and the person from the country, both are given, (eg Denmark; Danish, Dane).

To make the plural of a word for a person from a particular country, add -s, except for swiss and words ending in -ese (eg Japanese), which stay the same, and for words that end in -man or -woman, which change to -men or -women (eg three Frenchmen).
