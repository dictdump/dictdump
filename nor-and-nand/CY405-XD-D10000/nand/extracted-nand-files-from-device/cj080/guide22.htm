Aims


The OSD database provides an impressive array of real-world examples for the words, senses, and phrases to be found in the ODE. These help to illustrate such features as:


･ the contexts in which a word is normally used;

･ phrases, idioms, and constructions in which a word typically appears;

･ collocations;

･ differences in meaning between words or senses;

･ more subtle nuances of meaning, connotation, register, etc.


This will be of great value to users in enabling them to use words appropriately and idiomatically, and to understand linguistic and cultural subtleties beyond the scope of a conventional dictionary. The project is therefore particularly suited for supporting the needs of non-native speakers and high-level learners and ideal for teachers and lecturers in English.
