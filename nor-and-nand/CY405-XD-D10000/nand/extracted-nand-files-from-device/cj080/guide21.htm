What is the Oxford Sentence Dictionary?


The Oxford Sentence Dictionary (OSD) is a ground-breaking and unique electronic dictionary, forming a major new English language resource for a range of applications. It was developed by Oxford University Press and its creation was only possible because of the unrivalled quality and depth of Oxford's data resources.


The OSD links words, senses, and phrases in Oxford's most comprehensive current English dictionary, the Oxford Dictionary of English (ODE), to a selection of sentences, taken from the Oxford English Corpus (OEC), a 2-billion word database of 21st century English.


The OSD examples are intended to give the user a wide range of additional information to help them gain a better understanding of the word or sense in question and the ways in which it can be used. The OSD is particularly suited for supporting the needs of non-native speakers, enabling them to use words appropriately and idiomatically, and to understand linguistic and cultural subtleties beyond the scope of a conventional dictionary.
