(4)その他の文法コード

sb(someone)　主語･目的語に人が来ることを表す｡

sth(something)　主語･目的語に物が来ることを表す｡

[+ that]　that節を伴うことを表す｡

&F046;She allowed that the matter was serious.

[+ (that)]　thatが省略されることがある｡

&F046;Are you certain that it’s finished? or Are you certain it’s finished?

...to do sth　不定詞を伴って用いる｡

&F046;I love to go shopping.

...to doing sth　動名詞を伴って用いる｡

&F046;I love swimming.

[usually passive]　通例受け身形で用いる｡

&F046;It was an old-fashioned club from which women were barred.

[never passive]　受け身形では用いない｡

&F046;Can I have another piece of delicious cake?

[usually progressive]　通例進行形で用いる｡

&F046;What are you getting at?

[never progressive]　進行形では用いない｡

&F046;The room have a balcony facing the ocean.

[in imperative]　通例命令法で用いる｡

&F046;Let ABC be a triangle.

[in infinitive]　通例不定詞形で用いる｡

[in negatives or questions]　通例否定文または疑問文で用いる｡

&F046;You can go outside and play, but don’t go far.
