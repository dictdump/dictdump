(2)動詞の文法コード

[I](intransitive)　自動詞　直接目的語を取らない動詞｡

&F046;He paused for a moment. / Could you speak up please?

[T](transitive)　他動詞　直接目的語を取る動詞｡

&F046;I ate my lunch. / She handed the note over to me.

[linking verb]　連結動詞　第2文型SVCで用いられる動詞｡

&F046;They looked happy. / I feel better now.

[auxiliary verb]　助動詞　進行形,完了形などで用いられる be, do, have｡

&F046;When are you leaving? / They didn’t understand.

[modal verb]　法助動詞　can, may, must, will, shall など｡

&F046;She might come. / He can go now. / I will ask him to call you.

(3)形容詞の文法コード

[only before noun]　限定的用法(attributive use)　名詞の前でのみ用いられる形容詞｡

&F046;my elder sister

[never before noun]　叙述的用法(predicative use)　名詞の前では使われない形容詞｡

&F046;I felt afraid.
