4. HOW TO DIVIDE THE CHARACTER


1. Divide the character into two parts at the first division point


&F16F;1 Going from left to right, divide at the first space

-------------------------

Right　　明　　小　　扱

　　　　4－4　1－2　3－3

Wrong　　小　　街

　　　　2－1　9－3

-------------------------

&F16E;2 Going from top to bottom, divide at the first space, horizontal line, or frame element, whichever comes first

-------------------------

　　　　 三　　脅　　赤

Right　 1－2　2－8　3－4

Wrong　 2－1　6－4　2－5

-------------------------

&F16D;3 Going from the outside toward inside, divide after the first enclosure element

-------------------------

Right　　度　　進　　閉

　　　　3－6　3－8　8－3

Wrong　　度　　磨

　　　　7－2 11－5

-------------------------

2. Do not violate the principle of element integrity


1) Never break through stokes

-------------------------

Right　　凶　&F16D;3－2－2

Wrong　　凶　&F16F;1－1－4

-------------------------

2) Never break through indivisible units

-------------------------

Right　　情　&F16F;1－3－8

Wrong　　情　&F16F;1－1－10

-------------------------
