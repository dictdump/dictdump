2. ELECTRONIC DICTIONARY FORMAT


The Electronic Dictionary (ED) edition of The Kodansha Kanji Learner's Dictionary (KALD) is the latest addition to KIT (Kanji Integrated Tools) -- a series of computer-edited dictionaries and other useful learning aids for the effective mastery of the Japanese script. So far, the following KIT dictionaries have been published:


1. New Japanese-English Character Dictionary　NJECD

　 Kenkyusha　1990　Japanesemarket edition

2. New Japanese-English Character Dictionary　NJECD-NTC

　 NTC　1993　InternationalEdition

3. New Japanese-English Character Dictionary　NJECD-EB

　 Nichigai Associates　1995　Electronic BookEdition

4. The Kodansha Kanji Learner's Dictionary　　KALD

　 Kodansha International 1999　Learner's Edition

5. The Kodansha Kanji Learner's Dictionary　　KALD-ED

　 CASIO　2006　Electronic Dictionary Edition


Many learners and teachers throughout the world that have used KALD and NJECD have continuously been requesting that it be published in electronic dictionary format. In 1995 NJECD was published in Electronic Book format, a format that is now on the verge of extinction. Finally, with the strong support from Prof. Tomoharu Yanagimachi, associate professor at International Student Center, Hokkaido University , where all students and teachers use KALD (and have published a book on how to use KALD effectively), Casio Computer Co., Ltd. has made this a reality by publishing the ED edition of KALD.


The ED edition is essentially identical to the book edition of KALD, which in turn is based on the first installment of the series, the NTC/Kenkyusha New Japanese-English Character Dictionary (NJECD). This series of dictionaries have become established in Japanese language education circles as a standard reference works, and has enjoyed the praise and support of world-renowned scholars, educational institutions, and countless learners of Japanese all over the world.
