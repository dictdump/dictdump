2. HOW TO LOCATE AN ENTRY


DETERMINE THE SKIP NUMBER OF YOUR CHARACTER.


STEP 1: IDENTIFY PATTERN

Determine to which of the four patterns your character belongs to get the first part of the SKIP number (the pattern number).


&F041;DIVISIBLE CHARACTERS&F042;


STEP 2: DIVIDE CHARACTER

Divide the character into two parts at the first division point.


STEP 3: COUNT STROKES OF SHADED PART

Count the strokes of the shaded part to get the second part of the SKIP number.


STEP 4: COUNT STROKES OF BLANK PART

Count the strokes of the blank part to get the third part of the SKIP number.


&F041;INDIVISIBLE CHARACTERS&F042;


STEP 2: OMIT

(Since solid characters cannot be divided, go to STEP 3.


STEP 3: DETERMINE TOTAL STROKE-COUNT

Determine the total stroke-count of your character to get the second part of the SKIP number.


STEP 4: IDENTIFY SOLID SUBPATTERN

Determine to which of the four solid subpatterns your character belongs to get the third part of the SKIP number. Select from: &F16A;1, &F16B;2, &F16C;3, or &F368;4.
